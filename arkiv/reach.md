# Reach

## Who is it for

Crypto traders who follow call-makers on X and Telegram, and the call-makers themselves. Today a
follower has no way to know whether a caller's "I called ETH at 3,000" is true or backfilled.
Supercall turns every call into a public, uneditable Arkiv record with a real settled outcome, so a
caller builds a track record they can't fake and a follower can audit.
